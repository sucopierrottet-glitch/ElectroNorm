Privacy Policy for ElectroNorm Pro

Application: ElectroNorm Pro (com.simpleappspg.electronorm)
Developer: SimpleApps PG (simpleappspg)
Effective Date: October 13, 2026

100% Offline-First Architecture Guarantee: ElectroNorm Pro operates completely on your local device. We do not operate external databases, tracking servers, or telemetry services. Your electrical calculations, conduit schedules, and digital signatures remain strictly on your device.

1. Introduction & Overview

SimpleApps PG ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy discloses the privacy practices for the ElectroNorm Pro mobile application for electrical engineers, contractors, and inspectors. ElectroNorm Pro is designed from the ground up as an offline-first engineering utility that functions in remote substations, industrial plants, and mechanical rooms without network connectivity.

2. Information Collection and Telemetry

We believe in absolute data minimization:

  • Zero Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, transmit, sell, or store your name, email, physical location, or contact details on any remote server.
  • Zero Analytics & Tracking: ElectroNorm Pro does not contain Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Facebook SDK, or any third-party behavioral telemetry SDKs.
  • Zero Advertising Networks: There are no advertisements, ad networks, or ad measurement identifiers embedded in ElectroNorm Pro.

3. Local On-Device Storage

ElectroNorm Pro utilizes sandboxed local storage (SharedPreferences and internal app directory storage) exclusively on your device for:

  • Saved Conduit Runs: Raceway profiles, conductor schedules, wire groupings, voltage drop matrices, and derating calculations that you choose to save.
  • Technician & Company Profile: Electrician name, company branding, and state license number used to populate generated PDF compliance reports.
  • Subscription & Preference State: Local subscription status, free trial activation timestamps, and unit preferences.

This data is stored within your device's private application sandbox and is not accessible by other applications.

4. Digital Signatures & PDF Generation

ElectroNorm Pro provides an on-device digital signature pad allowing technicians and Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) inspectors to sign compliance reports:

  • Signature strokes are converted to vector images directly in device memory.
  • The signature is embedded locally into the generated PDF document.
  • Signatures are never transmitted to cloud servers, external verification services, or third-party storage.

5. Android Device Permissions

ElectroNorm Pro adheres strictly to the Principle of Least Privilege. We request only the following standard Android permission:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required solely for in-app subscription validation via Google Play Billing, checking store connectivity, and allowing users to view this Privacy Policy via their external browser.

ElectroNorm Pro does NOT request or require any high-risk or sensitive permissions, including:

  • Zero Camera permissions (CAMERA)
  • Zero Location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)
  • Zero Audio/Microphone permissions (RECORD_AUDIO)
  • Zero Contacts permissions (READ_CONTACTS)
  • Zero Shared Storage permissions (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)

7. Data Retention, Control & Deletion

You maintain complete control over your data at all times:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete any saved project run at any time using the delete icon or swipe-to-delete gesture within the Saved Projects screen.
  • Immediate Local Wipe: Uninstalling ElectroNorm Pro or clearing the app storage cache in your Android system settings immediately and permanently deletes all saved calculations, profiles, and cached reports from your device.

8. Children's Privacy

ElectroNorm Pro is an electrical engineering tool intended for trade professionals, inspectors, and contractors. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age, complying fully with COPPA and GDPR regulations.
